CounterStore is @Injectable({ providedIn: 'root' }). The two grey cards inject it directly → they share the root singleton. The two purple cards are wrapped in a component with providers: [CounterStore] → each gets its own instance. Remove them and watch the log: the service's ngOnDestroy(), its DestroyRef.onDestroy() callback and its takeUntilDestroyed() subscription all fire.
