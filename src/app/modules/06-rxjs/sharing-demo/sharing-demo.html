Cold vs hot: a cold Observable re-runs its producer for each subscriber (that's why two | async on the same http.get() make two requests). shareReplay(1) multicasts one execution and replays the last value to late subscribers.

Combination: combineLatest waits for every source to emit once, then emits on every change; withLatestFrom only emits when a$ emits; forkJoin waits for all to complete.
