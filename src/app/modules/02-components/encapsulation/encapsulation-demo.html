I am a plain <p> in the PARENT template: the None component's styles reached me.

Open DevTools: Emulated elements carry _ngcontent-*/_nghost-* attributes; the ShadowDom box has a #shadow-root. Note that the None styles also reach inside the shadow root: Angular adds the styles it manages (component styles) to every shadow root it creates — only the global stylesheet is left out. ExperimentalIsolatedShadowDom (v21+) exists to prevent this.
